# BillTrail: Design v2

> Read this, question it, and change anything you disagree with. You own this document.
> Money is always stored as an integer in the smallest unit (kobo). Never floats.

## 1. What BillTrail is

BillTrail lets a small business create professional invoices, send them to clients by email,
and get paid online through Paystack. The invoice status updates automatically when the
payment succeeds, and the business keeps a permanent record.

**Flow, end to end**

1. A business owner signs up and verifies their email.
2. They add clients (name, email, phone). Clients are contacts, not user accounts.
3. They create an invoice: pick a client, add line items, due date. It starts as a `DRAFT`.
4. They click Send. The server emails the client a link containing a secret token.
5. The client opens the link (no signup), sees the invoice, and clicks Pay.
6. The client pays on Paystack's checkout page.
7. Paystack calls our webhook. We verify it, mark the invoice `PAID`, and record the payment.
8. The business dashboard shows revenue, outstanding, and overdue amounts.

## 2. Actors and their rights

| Actor | Has an account? | Can do |
|-------|-----------------|--------|
| Business owner | Yes | Manage **only their own** business, clients, and invoices |
| Client | No | Open one invoice via its secret link, and pay it |
| Paystack | n/a | Call the webhook to report payment results |
| Anyone else | No | Nothing (no public lists of users, clients, or invoices) |

Every database query for business data is scoped by the `businessId` taken from the
authenticated token, never from the request body or URL alone.

## 3. Entities (PostgreSQL)

Relationships live in the child table as a foreign key. There are no arrays of IDs.

**User**
- id (uuid), email (unique), passwordHash, emailVerifiedAt, createdAt, updatedAt

**Business** (one per User for now)
- id, ownerId -> User (unique), name, phone, address, currency (default "NGN"), createdAt

**Client**
- id, businessId -> Business, name, email, phone, deletedAt (soft delete), createdAt, updatedAt

**Invoice**
- id, businessId -> Business, clientId -> Client
- number (unique per business, e.g. INV-0001)
- status: DRAFT | SENT | PAID | CANCELLED
- currency
- subtotal, taxAmount, discountAmount, total (all integers in kobo, calculated by the server)
- issueDate, dueDate
- publicToken (long random string, used in the payment link)
- sentAt, viewedAt, paidAt, cancelledAt
- notes, createdAt, updatedAt

**InvoiceItem**
- id, invoiceId -> Invoice, description, quantity (integer), unitPrice (kobo), lineTotal (kobo, calculated by the server)

**Payment**
- id, invoiceId -> Invoice, amount (kobo), currency
- status: PENDING | SUCCESS | FAILED
- reference (unique, generated by us and sent to Paystack)
- providerTransactionId (nullable, filled from Paystack)
- paidAt, createdAt

**WebhookEvent** (for idempotency)
- id, provider, eventId (unique), type, payload (json), processedAt

Relationships: Business has many Clients. Client has many Invoices. Invoice has many
InvoiceItems and many Payments (a failed attempt followed by a successful one).

## 4. Invoice status rules

Stored statuses: `DRAFT`, `SENT`, `PAID`, `CANCELLED`.
Derived (not stored): **viewed** = `viewedAt` is set. **Overdue** = status is `SENT` and `dueDate` is in the past.

| From | To | Triggered by |
|------|----|--------------|
| DRAFT | SENT | Owner clicks Send (server emails the link) |
| DRAFT | (deleted) | Owner deletes a draft |
| SENT | PAID | Verified Paystack webhook only |
| SENT | CANCELLED | Owner cancels |

Rules:
- Only `DRAFT` invoices can be edited or deleted.
- A `SENT` invoice cannot have its amounts changed. To fix a mistake: cancel it and create a new one.
- A `PAID` invoice is permanent: no edits, no deletes. This is the audit and tax record.
- Status is never accepted from a request body. It changes only through specific actions
  (`send`, `cancel`) or the verified webhook.
- Totals and line totals are always calculated by the server. The client sends quantities and unit prices only.
- Overdue invoices stay visible and show up as debt on the dashboard.

## 5. API (v1)

**Auth**
- POST /auth/register
- POST /auth/verify-email
- POST /auth/login
- POST /auth/refresh
- POST /auth/logout
- POST /auth/forgot-password
- POST /auth/reset-password

**Business** (authenticated)
- GET /business/me
- PATCH /business/me

**Clients** (authenticated, own business only)
- POST /clients
- GET /clients (paginated)
- GET /clients/:id
- PATCH /clients/:id
- DELETE /clients/:id (soft delete)

**Invoices** (authenticated, own business only)
- POST /invoices (invoice and its line items in one request)
- GET /invoices (filters: status, clientId, page)
- GET /invoices/:id
- PATCH /invoices/:id (drafts only)
- DELETE /invoices/:id (drafts only)
- POST /invoices/:id/send
- POST /invoices/:id/cancel

**Public** (no login; secured by the secret token)
- GET /public/invoices/:token (also sets viewedAt)
- POST /public/invoices/:token/pay (creates a Payment, initializes Paystack, returns the checkout URL)

**Webhook**
- POST /webhooks/paystack

**Dashboard** (authenticated)
- GET /dashboard/summary

## 6. Security rules

- Ownership check on every business query (scope by `businessId`).
- Validate every request body with Zod. Accept only the fields we expect.
- Never return `passwordHash` or tokens in any response.
- Webhook: verify the `x-paystack-signature` header (HMAC SHA512 of the raw body with our secret key),
  check that the amount and currency match the invoice, and skip events we have already processed.
- Rate limit auth and public endpoints. Use helmet and a restricted CORS origin.
- Secrets live in environment variables, and we never log them.

## 7. Out of scope for v1

Recurring invoices, PDF export, team members and roles, partial payments, client accounts,
credit notes, multiple currencies.
